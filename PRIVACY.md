# YouTube Buttons Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

YouTube Buttons is developed by Brandyn. It adds playback speed, screenshot, audio, and player customisation controls to YouTube. The developer does not receive your browsing activity, video content, audio, screenshots, or saved preferences.

## Information used by the extension

The extension accesses the current YouTube player and page information to provide its features. This includes playback state, autoplay state, the current page address, and the video title used to name a screenshot. It does not maintain a browsing-history log.

Audio compression, equalizer, and volume boost process audio locally in your browser. The extension does not record or upload audio.

When you request a screenshot, the extension captures the current video frame and downloads a PNG to your device. Temporary image data is released after use. Screenshots are not uploaded or included in settings sync.

## Saved preferences and browser sync

The extension stores preferences locally, including toolbar visibility and order, playback speed and step, audio settings, colours, and compact player controls. It also remembers the last extension-selected playback speed and audio-tweaks toggle state.

These preferences are also written to the browser's built-in extension sync storage. If you have enabled the relevant browser-account sync, Google Chrome can synchronize them through your Google account, and desktop Firefox can synchronize them through your Mozilla account. This allows preferences to be restored on other computers or after reinstalling. Chrome and Firefox use separate sync services.

The developer does not operate a sync server and cannot access your browser account or synced preferences. Browser-managed synchronization is subject to the applicable [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) or [Mozilla Privacy Notice](https://www.mozilla.org/privacy/firefox/).

## Sharing, analytics, and advertising

The extension does not send information to the developer and contains no analytics, advertising, or tracking services. It does not sell user data or use it for advertising, creditworthiness, or lending decisions. Preferences are provided to the browser's sync service solely to support settings synchronization.

The use of user data is limited to the extension's described features, consistent with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

## Storage and user choices

Preferences remain in extension storage until changed or removed. You can change them in the extension popup. You can manage browser-account synchronization through your browser's settings. Removing the extension removes its local extension storage; copies held by the browser's sync service are governed by that service's retention and deletion controls. Downloaded screenshots remain on your device until you delete them.

## Permissions

- YouTube site access integrates controls with YouTube pages and supported embedded players.
- Storage saves preferences locally and through browser-managed sync.
- Alarms schedules batched settings saves, reducing unnecessary writes. It does not periodically collect browsing information.

## Changes and contact

This policy will be updated if the extension's data handling changes. The date above identifies the latest revision.

For privacy questions, contact Brandyn through the [YouTube Buttons issue tracker](https://github.com/brandyyn/YouTube-Buttons/issues). Information you choose to post there is handled by GitHub and may be public.
